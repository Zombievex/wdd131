Runettic Lattice

Lattice Structure

While many groups have part of a lattice, the full Runettic Latice can be split in two major sections. The single haxagon lattice holds one path to power, and each of the three paths (Power from within, power from beyond, and remnant power) has their own Lattice, which you can see in the large lattice. Each Path lattice has the same structure.

Runettic users, assuming they have access to runes, can control their powers by pulling energy from their path, and then controlling that energy as shown in each circle. Each Path lattice has six ways to control that energy. Most users are proficient in one of the six ways of controls, but they can also stretch themselves to another form of control. This is a lot easier if the control circles are connected (as shown by the line) so eat person realistically has access to five control methods

Path of Self

The path of self focuses on drawing energy from within. Since the energy is already compatible with the body, those who follow the path of self usually get stronger faster. Unfortunately, pulling energy from one's own body has health complications, most users don't live very long, and their energy reserves dry up quickly.

Body

Body control allows a user to greatly enhance their strength, sPeed and every other physical attribute. This puts immense strain on the body, especially for lond term. Certain paths allow the user to store their strength, bypassing the permanent damage to thier body, but they have to spend time storing that energy, temporarily putting them at a weaker state.

Infuse

Infusion control allows the user to connect themselves to a physical object. They can enhance the object much like how Body control enhances a body. Many users of Infuse control also give their object lots of other uses, and can do things like grow bigger, or even other forms of control like using Burst to launch energy at someone.

Mind

Mind control allows the user to enhance their own thoughts, helping them think deeper, and sorting through and pulling up memories. THe longer someone uses this control, the better the effects.

Forge

Forge control allows the user to create objects from energy. Most objects created are temporary, but all can be enhanced in different ways from infusion. Users have been seen duplicating their body, creating invisible weapons, and even creating artificial life.

Spirit

Spirit control allows one to see things in a different way from normal intelligence. While not many know examples, we've seen user's tell how well they will get along with others, see their own potential, and keep themselves alive from a fatal blow by tansfering thier spirit to another body.

Burst

Burst Control Allows a user to launch energy out. Despite it's name, this can be either all at once, or a continuous release of energy. Most effective Bursts involve changing the energy, like making it come out as fire or ice.

Path of Beyond

The path of beyond focuses on drawing and controlling energy outside of one's own body. The user can either draw the energy into themselves for use, or move the energy outside themselves. While users take longer to get stronger compared to Path of Self, they can fight near continuously.

Body

Body control allows a user to draw energy from their surroundings into their own body. Some users specifies a specific energy type, which greatly helps in absorbing energy, like gaining a lot of energy from fire or light. Users can use this energy to strengthen themselves, move faster, and even extend their lifespan.

Infuse

Infusion control allows the user change physical objects, and let the object gather energy from it's surroundings. Many long-term structures can last near indefinately if infused right. Also has been shown To create a bomb that gets stronger over time.

Mind

Mind control allows the user to pull information from their surroundings. The user can learn things they didn't already know, and in some cases can even lead to mind reading. Extreme cases results in a user losing control, and absorbing more information then they can process, since this doesn't help the user with the information they already have.

Forge

Forge control allows the user to create objects from other objects around them, and "forge" those objects together into one. A popular use for this is creating golems of various materials, or even superior battle armor.

Spirit

Spirit control allows one to see things in a different way from normal intelligence. This allows the user to see other's potential, tell if someone is lying, and in extreme cases, attack the users spirit, killing them without leaving wounds on their body.

Burst

Burst Control allows a user to move and control the energy around them. This allows users to raise or extinquish flames, mold the earth into various shapes, control the flow of water, and so much more. This power has great power over other paths that try to use Burst Control.

Remnant Path

The Remnant path focuses on drawing energy from things that have once lived. Due to the nature of the path, many followers are known to be extremely violent to further their own strangth. This path allows great versatility, but they need a supply of dead matter.

Body

Body control allows a user to attach dead body parts to their own. Many users will kill other people for extra arms, or sometimes they even move around thier body parts. Users have been seen replacing organs with more durable replacements from beasts.

Infuse

Infusion control allows the user to connect dead parts to objects. Infusing control also allows the user to connect dead parts to other living creates, at the cost of their sanity. Many horrible monstrosities are born from this practice.

Mind

Mind Control, quite simply, allows the users to pull information, usually from drinking blood. This can also work by drinking living creatues.

Forge

Forge control allows the user to reanimate bodies of the dead. The reanimated dead can either be directly controller, or set loose with a simple command to follow, like destroy.

Spirit

Not much is known from Spirit control using the remnant path, except that users can still the soul energy from those they kill, making themselves much harder to die. This isn't as useful as it sounds, since it doesn't make you body any more durable, it just makes you're spirit less likely to leave your body.

Burst

Burst Control allows the user to launch energy from body parts, or make body parts explode. Can also be used to launch the users own blood as a weapon, but this can lead to health problems.
