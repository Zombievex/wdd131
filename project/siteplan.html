Runettic

I chose this name because the type of magic system featured inside is called Runettic, which is the process of using runes (rune-) and shapes (-ettic) to do things from strengthening your body to attacking.

Site Purpose

To explain the magic system featured in a fictional book series, helping readers understand the paths that the main characters take in order to strengthen themselves, as well as push into new discoveries.

Scenarios

  • Why are runes sometimes used, and sometimes ignored, by characters in the book?
  • What purpose do the shapes serve in the magic diagram? Why do lines connect the different nodes into shapes?
